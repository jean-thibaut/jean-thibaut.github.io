We consider a class of spacetimes endowed with non-abelian translational symmetries, motivated by Snyder geometries and related noncommutative spacetime constructions. Working within the framework of Cartan geometry, we introduce a corresponding notion of non-abelian frame fields, and study the induced deformed topological gravitational action. The resulting theory generates additional interaction terms mixing curvature and torsion. We show that these modifications lead to a deformation of the gravitational field equations in which torsion acquires a direct contribution to Einstein’s equations.  The equations of motion for the spin connection are modified by a cosmological constant-like term, while additional curvature-torsion mixing terms render these into differential equations for both torsion and curvature. As a consequence, spin density now sources both dynamical torsion and curvature. Even in the absence of matter sources this leads to an interaction between torsion and curvature.
